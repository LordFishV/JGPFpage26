1. Visual Hierarchy & Clarity

Guiding the User's Eye

Visual hierarchy organizes content so users naturally notice the most important elements first through contrast, size, and layout alignment.

Typography & Scale

Using distinct font scales creates predictable reading paths across long pages.

Font Weight Contrast

Bold titles combined with regular body text clearly separate headings from paragraphs.

Minor Sub-caption Example

Smallest heading level used for minor annotations or inline sub-sections.

2. Inclusive & Accessible Design

Designing for Everyone

Accessible web design ensures people with varying abilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with the site seamlessly.

Design Quick Tips

  • Maintain consistent whitespace padding.
  • Ensure high color contrast ratios.
  • Design mobile-first layouts.
